1954

The English translation of Jean Piaget’s “The Construction of Reality in the Child” is published. Originally published in French in 1937, the book explains how children actively construct an understanding of the world through interaction with their environment (Piaget, 1954).

1960

Jerome Bruner publishes “The Process of Education,” describing a spiral curriculum in which learners revisit fundamental concepts with increasing complexity, building on their existing understanding (Bruner, 1960).

1976

David Wood, Jerome Bruner, and Gail Ross publish “The Role of Tutoring in Problem Solving,” introducing scaffolding to describe how a tutor supports a learner in completing a task beyond the learner’s independent abilities (Wood et al., 1976).

1978

Lev Vygotsky’s “Mind in Society” is published posthumously in English. The collection presents his earlier work on socially mediated learning and the zone of proximal development, providing foundations for social constructivist approaches (Vygotsky, 1978).

1989

John Seely Brown, Allan Collins, and Paul Duguid publish “Situated Cognition and the Culture of Learning,” arguing that knowledge is connected to the activities, contexts, and cultures in which it develops. Their work supports authentic tasks and learning through participation in practice (Brown et al., 1989).
